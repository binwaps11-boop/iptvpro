 CONCLUSION (no spin):
 - Layers 1, 2, 3 ARE 30 on this build, on every 2.4G channel 1..11:
   Requested=30, Driver Applied=30 (PA pushed to max), PHY Advertised=30.0.
   Persists after reboot / wifi reload (baked into the image).
 - Layer 4 (Real RF / EIRP) is NOT proven 30. The QCA9550 integrated 2.4GHz
   PA is physically limited to ~25-26 dBm clean conducted; forcing "30" clips.
   This file does NOT claim a measured clean RF 30. That is why this image is
   named "system-30dbm-all-channels-extreme", NOT "real-30dbm".

 TO GET A REAL 30 (hardware, not software):
 - 30 dBm CONDUCTED  -> external power amplifier (PA).
 - 30 dBm EIRP       -> higher-gain antenna, e.g. 24 dBm TX + 6 dBi - 0 dB
                        cable = 30 dBm EIRP, NO clipping (best for a product).
 - 5GHz already does a real, clean 30 dBm (QCA9880 + ch149).

 state conducted-vs-EIRP + antenna gain + cable loss.
