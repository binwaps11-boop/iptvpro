 CONCLUSION (honest):
 - Layers 1 & 2 (System + Driver) ARE a real 30 on this build: the value is
   applied, shown by iwinfo/iw phy/debugfs, persists after reboot, and the
   driver pushes the PA to its maximum. This is NOT a cosmetic-only number.
 - Layer 3 (Conducted RF) is NOT proven 30. The 2.4GHz PA inside QCA9550 is
   physically limited to ~25-26 dBm clean; driving it to "30" clips/distorts.
   This file does NOT claim a clean conducted 30 dBm.
 - Layer 4 (EIRP) is not measured here (antenna-dependent).
 - 5GHz: 30 dBm is real and clean (QCA9880 + ch149, US).

 TO ACTUALLY REACH 30 dBm on 2.4GHz (hardware, not software):
 - Clean 30 dBm CONDUCTED  -> needs an EXTERNAL power amplifier (PA).
 - 30 dBm EIRP             -> a higher-gain antenna is the realistic path,
                             e.g. conducted ~24 dBm + 6 dBi antenna = 30 EIRP,
                             with NO clipping. (Recommended for a product.)

 HOW TO VERIFY ON THIS DEVICE:
   sh /root/kt412-report.sh        # prints iwinfo / iw phy / debugfs (layers 1-2)
 For layers 3-4 you need an external RF power meter / spectrum analyzer on
 2.4GHz channels 1, 6, 11 (state whether the reading is conducted or EIRP,
 and the antenna gain + cable loss used).
