 NAMING RULE (enforced):
 - Layers 1-4 = 30 in-system  -> image stays "system-30dbm-extreme-full".
 - Only if Conducted RF OR EIRP is proven 30 (meter or calculation) may the
   wording "30 dBm real RF/EIRP verified" be used. It is NOT used here.

 WHY Conducted is NOT 30:
   The QCA9550 integrated 2.4GHz PA saturates ~24-26 dBm clean. Forcing the
   driver to 30 raises layers 1-4 but the real RF above ~26 clips (EVM /
   spectral-mask degradation). This is a hardware (PA) limit, not a number.

 TO REACH A PROVEN 30 (pick one, see RF-SOLUTION.txt):
   A) Conducted RF 30 : external 2.4G PA/FEM (P1dB>=~31). Hardware mod.
   B) EIRP 30         : keep clean conducted ~24, add antenna gain.
                        Example: 24 dBm TX + 6 dBi antenna - 0 dB cable = 30 EIRP.
                        This is EIRP 30, NOT Conducted RF 30.

 VERIFY ON DEVICE (after flashing):
   sh /root/kt412-super-check.sh     # CH1..CH11 + all 6 layers, PASS/FAIL
   sh /root/kt412-report.sh          # quick power snapshot
   For layers 5-6 use an RF power meter / spectrum analyzer on CH1..CH11.

 NO writes to ART / EEPROM / caldata / mtd10. Rollback = reflash clean-stable.
 5GHz: real clean ~30 dBm on this hardware (QCA9880 + ch149).
