==========================================================================
 KT412 2.4GHz — HOW TO REACH A REAL 30 dBm (RF / EIRP), not just System 30
 Engineering options, honest, repeatable on other devices.
==========================================================================
 Chip: QCA9550 integrated 2.4GHz radio (ath9k). Clean conducted ceiling ~25-26
 dBm (PA saturation). System/Driver can be forced to 30, but RF above ~26 clips.

 --- The 4 power terms (keep separate) ---
   System Power     = what uci/config requests        (this build: 30)
   Driver Power     = what ath9k applies (debugfs)    (this build: 30, PA maxed)
   Conducted RF     = real power out of the chip pin  (~25-26 clean, NOT 30)
   EIRP             = Conducted + AntennaGain - CableLoss  (radiated total)

 Q1: Can the SAME chip give a clean Conducted RF 30?
     NO. The QCA9550 internal PA saturates ~25-26 dBm. Pushing 30 = clipping,
     EVM degradation, spectral-mask issues, heat, often WORSE real range.

 Q2: Do we need an external PA?
     For a clean CONDUCTED 30: YES. An external 2.4GHz PA (FEM) after the radio.

 Q3: What kind of external PA?
     A 2.4GHz front-end module / PA with ~+6-8 dB gain and P1dB >= ~31 dBm,
     linear for 802.11 (e.g. a Skyworks/Qorvo 2.4G WLAN FEM). Must include
     proper bias + harmonic filtering. Adds cost + board rework + heat.

 Q4: Where in the RF path?
     radio TX pin -> [matching] -> EXTERNAL PA -> band-pass filter -> antenna.
     (RX path needs an LNA/bypass + T/R switch — a full FEM handles both.)
     This is a HARDWARE modification; not possible by firmware.

 Q5: Do we need to change the antenna?
     This is the CHEAP, SAFE, repeatable route for EIRP (not conducted):
     a higher-gain antenna raises EIRP without touching the PA (no clipping).

 Q6: Antenna gain needed for 30 dBm EIRP?
     EIRP = Conducted + AntennaGain - CableLoss.
     With the chip's clean conducted ~24 dBm:
        need AntennaGain >= 30 - 24 + CableLoss.
     With ~0 dB cable loss -> a 6 dBi antenna.

 Q7: Final formula:
        EIRP(dBm) = Conducted_TX(dBm) + Antenna_Gain(dBi) - Cable_Loss(dB)

 Q8: Worked example (acceptable, clean, no clipping):
        24 dBm TX  +  6 dBi antenna  -  0 dB cable  =  30 dBm EIRP

 --- BEST COMMERCIAL / REPEATABLE SOLUTION (recommendation) ---
   Use the chip at its CLEAN conducted power (~24 dBm) and reach 30 dBm EIRP
   with a 5-6 dBi antenna. This is:
     * clean (no clipping/distortion), * stable (no PA stress/heat),
     * cheap (antenna only), * repeatable on any ath9k/QCA device,
     * legal where 30 dBm EIRP is permitted on 2.4GHz.
   Only use an external PA if you truly need higher CONDUCTED power into a
   long cable / splitter; it is costlier and must stay linear to avoid clipping.

 5GHz: already a clean conducted/EIRP 30 on this hardware (QCA9880 + ch149).
