==========================================================================
KT412 2.4GHz — RF SOLUTIONS: how to actually get more real output
(Not cosmetic numbers. Honest options, with the trade-offs.)
==========================================================================

Q1) Can 30 dBm CONDUCTED (clean) come from the same hardware?
    Per chain: NO. The external 2.4G PA on this board is calibrated clean at
    ~24 dBm and saturates (P1dB) around ~25-26 dBm. Above that the signal is
    compressed/dirty, not 30 dBm of usable power.
    2-chain aggregate: ~27-29 dBm conducted is realistic.

Q2) What is the real expected clean limit on THIS board?
    ~24-26 dBm per chain conducted.
    ~27-29 dBm aggregate (two chains combined).
    ~29-32 dBm EIRP after antenna gain.

Q3) Technical reason for the limit?
    External PA P1dB / EVM + 802.11 spectral mask. The EEPROM target table
    (24 dBm) encodes exactly that calibrated clean point. Secondary: thermal
    (Temp Slope=56 -> firmware backs off when the PA heats up). It is NOT a
    software/UCI limit — software already says 30.

Q4) What is REQUIRED for real 30 dBm conducted?
    a) External higher-power 2.4G PA / FEM with P1dB >= ~32 dBm (e.g. a 1-2 W
       2.4GHz PA module) on each chain, plus matched input drive and output
       filtering to keep the spectral mask legal. This is a HARDWARE mod.
    b) Adequate PA supply: a 1 W PA per chain draws significant current; the
       board's 3.3V/5V rail + regulator must support it (often the real
       bottleneck on retrofits).
    c) Heatsinking/airflow: sustained high PA output needs cooling or temp-comp
       will throttle it back down.
    d) Re-calibration of the new PA's target table (NOT on this unit's locked
       ART/EEPROM — you would need a dev board or a separate caldata path).
    Easier legal alternative if the GOAL is range, not conducted watts:
    e) Higher-gain antennas. +6 dBi antennas turn ~24 dBm conducted into
       ~30 dBm EIRP with a clean signal and no PA stress. This is the
       practical, regulation-friendly way to "reach 30".

Q5) Correct way to MEASURE (so numbers are real, not guessed):
    Conducted:
      - Disconnect the antenna; connect the chain's RF port through a 30 dB
        attenuator (or directional coupler) into an RF power meter or
        spectrum analyzer; transmit a CW/duty pattern; read dBm. Subtract the
        attenuator/cable loss. Do each chain.
    Spectral/EVM:
      - Spectrum analyzer with the 802.11 mask template; confirm no regrowth
        when you raise power. This is what proves "clean".
    EIRP:
      - Anechoic chamber or calibrated open-field measurement with a reference
        antenna at a known distance (Friis), or a 3D turntable for the pattern.
    Without at least an attenuator + power meter, any conducted dBm is an
    estimate, and we label it as such.

SUMMARY OF WHAT THIS FIRMWARE DELIVERS vs WHAT NEEDS HARDWARE
  Delivered now (software/driver):  iwinfo/iw phy/debugfs netdev = 30 dBm,
                                     chip commanded to 30 (real registers).
  Needs hardware for real RF 30:    bigger external PA + supply + cooling +
                                     recal, OR use +6 dBi antennas for 30 EIRP.
